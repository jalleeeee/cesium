Why This Property Is a Priority Sampling Target

The Illinois Furnace Fault Zone crosses Section 19 at N60°–65°E with documented 850-foot vertical displacement. Per the 2024 Terra Nova paper by Trela, Freiburg, Gazel et al. (doi:10.1111/ter.12712), this is precisely the type of brecciated structural conduit through which HREE-bearing brine-melts traveled from the deep carbonatite source during the Wauboukigou igneous event.

The property is 7.8 miles from the center of Hicks Dome along this same fault system. The Sparks Hill Diatreme — which produced confirmed REE concentrations of La:293 ppm, Ce:467 ppm, Nd:143 ppm — is approximately 3.1 miles from the property.

Critically, this property has NEVER been sampled for rare earth elements at any depth. The last drilling was the 1944 USGS program (USBM Report of Investigations 4315, Swanson), which targeted shallow Chesterian strata and predates modern REE geochemical methods by half a century.

Phase III sampling of this property would:

  • Test whether the REE transport described in the 2024 Terra Nova paper extended along the Illinois Furnace Fault Zone to the Interstate Subdistrict
  • Provide REE data for a structurally significant location between the known Sparks Hill occurrences and the Hicks Dome deposit
  • Potentially confirm a domestic REE resource in a property with clear fee simple ownership, active tax status, and willing owners ready to support exploration
