# Blueprint Upload Guide

Hover enables users to prepare takeoffs for new construction projects by uploading blueprints without manual scaling. Each structure or variation Hover builds at your request will be delivered and billed to your account as a separate project.

## File Requirements

**Accepted formats:** PDF, DWG, or DXF

**Required documentation:**

- **Elevation shots** — one for each side (front, back, left, right) showing exterior walls
- **Floor plans** — one per building level, with legible measurements
- **High resolution** — blueprints must be clear and non-blurry so measurements are readable

## Upload Process

1. Log into your account (web or mobile).
2. Click **"Choose a file"** or drag and drop files.
3. Accept the terms and conditions.
4. Click **"Upload and continue"**.

## Output Selection

After uploading, choose between two processing options:

### Everything from File

Hover processes all variations automatically, creating separate jobs for each. Hover will comb through the blueprint plan and select the volume of options to be created.

### Specify Details

For targeted requests, select:

- **Property size** — under or over 10,000 sq ft
- **Measurement type** — Complete or Roof-Only
- **Structure names** — must match blueprint labels exactly
- **Job notes** — optional, for additional instructions

## Pricing and Timeline

- **Cost:** Per-job pricing for blueprints starts as low as $55 before discounts.
- **Turnaround:** Most jobs return within 24 hours. Structures over 10,000 sq ft may take up to 48 hours.

## Supported Trades

Hover handles siding and roofing trades with detail for fascia, soffit, gutters, window openings, and more.
